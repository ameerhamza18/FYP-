# TrustLayer Test Suite - Scan Examples

## 1. SAFE EXAMPLES (Should be LOW risk)
- "Hi Sarah, are we still meeting at 3 PM for the project review?"
- "Your appointment with Dr. Smith is confirmed for tomorrow at 10 AM."
- "Check out the official documentation at https://docs.python.org"
- "The weather today is sunny with a high of 25 degrees."

## 2. SUSPICIOUS TEXT (Should be MEDIUM to HIGH risk)
- "URGENT: Your account has been compromised! Please verify your identity immediately by clicking the link below to avoid permanent suspension."
- "Congratulations! You've won a $1,000 Amazon gift card! Claim your prize now by providing your shipping address and credit card details for verification."
- "This is the IRS. You have an outstanding tax debt of $4,500. To avoid legal action and immediate arrest, call our agent at +1-800-555-0199 now."
- "Hey, I found your lost wallet! Just pay the courier fee of $20 via Bitcoin and I'll send it back to you immediately."

## 3. SUSPICIOUS URLS (Should be HIGH to CRITICAL risk)
- http://secure-bank-login-verify.com/update-password
- http://amaz0n-rewards-claim.net/free-gift
- http://paypal-account-security-alert.info/login
- http://microsoft-support-help-desk.xyz/ticket-12345
- http://bit.ly/suspicious-short-link-example

## 4. MIXED MESSAGES (Text + URL) (Should be HIGH to CRITICAL risk)
- "Your Netflix subscription has expired. Please update your payment method at http://netflix-payment-update.com to continue watching."
- "Package Delivery Alert: Your shipment #TRK99281 is held at our warehouse. Pay the $2.50 customs fee at http://postal-service-pay.top to receive your package."
